Questions

Frequently asked questions

Who can use StagNest?
Any current Fairfield University student with a @student.fairfield.edu email can create an account, save listings, and apply. Landlords can list a property with any valid email — their account is reviewed before it goes live.
Are the reviews really from verified tenants?
Yes. Only students who signed up with a verified Fairfield email and were connected to a lease on StagNest can leave a review, so what you're reading reflects real move-in experiences.
How do location ratings work?
Each listing shows walk times to campus, The Seagrape, restaurants, and grocery stores, plus safety, quiet, and convenience scores gathered from tenant feedback — so you know what a block is actually like, not just where it sits on a map.
Can I pay rent through StagNest?
Once you've signed a lease, your tenant portal lets you pay rent, split it automatically with roommates, and turn on autopay so you never miss a due date.
What if I need to report a landlord or a bad listing?
Every listing has a report option, and our team reviews flagged landlords and listings by hand — repeat offenders get removed from the platform.
