Questions

Frequently asked questions

Who can use StagNest?
Any current Fairfield student with a @student.fairfield.edu email — that's it, that's the bar. Create an account, save listings, apply, all of it. Landlords can sign up with any valid email, but their account gets reviewed by us before a single listing goes live.
Are the reviews really from verified tenants?
Yes — genuinely. Only students who verified with a real Fairfield email and were actually connected to a lease on StagNest can leave a review. What you're reading is a real move-in experience, not a five-star review from someone's cousin.
How do location ratings work?
Every listing shows walk times to campus, The Seagrape, restaurants, and the grocery store, plus safety, quiet, and convenience scores pulled straight from tenant feedback. You'll know what the block is actually like — not just where a pin drops on a map.
Can I pay rent through StagNest?
Once your lease is signed, yes. Your tenant portal handles rent payments, splits it automatically across roommates so nobody's chasing anybody on Venmo, and autopay's there if you'd rather never think about the due date again.
What if I need to report a landlord or a bad listing?
Every listing has a report button, right there, no digging required. A real person on our team reviews every flag by hand — and repeat offenders don't get a third chance. They're off the platform.
